About me

I received my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from the Faculty of Engineering in 2019, followed by an M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering (Instrumentation) from the Institute of Applied Sciences and Technology in 2021, graduating with honors. In 2026, I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering (Instrumentation) from the Institute of Engineering, also with honors. All three degrees were awarded by the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM). In 2024, I completed a research stay at INQUIMAE, University of Buenos Aires (UBA), Argentina. I have been involved in university teaching since [year]. My research interests include electrochemical and electrical sensors, electronic instrumentation, and semiconductor development, with a particular focus on technological solutions to challenges in energy, water, and healthcare. Since May 2026, I have been a Visiting Professor at Universidad Autónoma Metropolitana (UAM), Unidad Lerma, where I conduct research, teach undergraduate and graduate courses, supervise students, and contribute to technological development.
